Master's Thesis Research Project

Stockholm University, Sweden & University of Turin, Italy

2025

Supervisors: Prof. Jonas Larson (Stockholm University), Prof. Alberto Carlini (Univ. of Turin)

Project: Study of quantum walks on 1D lattices (standard 1D lattice and synthetic Fock-state 1D lattice) under various evolution dynamics.

Key Contributions & Technical Achievements:

  • Developed analytical and numerical models of generalized quantum walks on 1D lattices as simulation tools for many-body quantum systems.
  • Defined discrete-time quantum walks on 1D Fock-state lattices emerging from a two-mode detuned Jaynes-Cummings interaction.
  • Constructed a time-parameterized theoretical framework connecting discrete-time and continuous-time quantum walks.
  • Analyzed stochastic quantum-walk dynamics as open quantum systems, deriving effective Lindblad master equations $\dot{\rho} = -i[H,\rho] + \mathcal{L}(\rho)$ and quantifying decoherence, entanglement generation, and quantum transport via Python QuTiP simulations.

Resulted in two first-author research papers (one in Phys. Rev. A, one submitted to PRA).
